Our goal

We want everyone to be able to use this site. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA.

What we do

  • Pages use clear headings, text alternatives for charts and keyboard-accessible controls.
  • Colors are checked for contrast, and charts do not rely on color alone where we can avoid it.
  • Pages work on phones and with browser zoom.

Known limits

Some interactive charts are easier to use with a mouse or touch than with a screen reader. The underlying numbers are available in tables or on request.

Tell us

If something on the site is hard to use, email info@workforce2100.ai. We aim to reply within [5 business days] and will provide the content in another format.
