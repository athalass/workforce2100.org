A 10-question diagnostic for academic leaders

Is your AI program building literacy or fluency?

Most campus AI initiatives teach students to operate tools. Employers now need graduates who can decide what to delegate and prove the result is right. The two are different capabilities, built in different ways.

About this check. The questions put into practice the framework in The AI Fluency Imperative (Thalassinidis & Bowen, 2026): the four pillars of fluency (boundary design, sign-off by consequence, adversarial auditing, judgment before capability), and a two-stage model in which classroom friction builds foundations and consequence-bearing experiences, from clinical-model internships to client projects and student ventures, build judgment.

Your answers and privacy. Your score is calculated in your browser. Nothing is stored unless you choose to submit it in step 2 and agree to the statement there. Submitted responses include your answers and your institution's name, type, state and size, but not your name or email. We use them only in aggregate and never display or publish an individual institution's name or result. This is a conversation starter for leadership teams, not a validated instrument.
