Who runs this site

workforce2100.org is the personal research site of Angelo E. Thalassinidis. The research it presents is open: methods, papers and data are published for anyone to check and reuse. Papers written with co-authors are credited to all their authors.

Academic affiliations

I work at Anne Arundel Community College. The college does not sponsor, review or endorse this site unless stated. The survey studies listed here are overseen by the college's Institutional Review Board, as described on the research participation page.
