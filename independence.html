Who runs this site

workforce2100.org is operated by Horizon 2100 LLC. The research it presents is open: methods, papers and our own data are published for anyone to check and reuse.

Related commercial service

Horizon 2100 LLC also offers paid reports and services at workforce2100.ai that apply the methods published here to specific states, regions and institutions. This site links to that service from its data pages and footer, in clearly labeled notes; survey and research-participation pages carry no such links. To keep the research independent:

  • Research findings on this site are not changed to suit any client.
  • Clients do not receive early or exclusive access to the open research or data.
  • Client data and client reports are not published here without the client's permission.
  • Methods used in paid work are the methods published here.

Academic affiliations

Some of our researchers hold academic appointments. Their institutions do not sponsor, review or endorse this site or Horizon 2100 LLC's commercial services unless stated.

Funding

The research is currently unfunded. Any future grants or sponsors will be listed here.
