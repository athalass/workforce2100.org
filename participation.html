Our surveys

The surveys on this site are research studies. Each one explains its purpose, what it asks, how long it takes and how results are reported before you begin.

  • The Fluency Check: for academic leaders, about 5 minutes.
  • Expert review: Calibrated Delegation Assessment: for experts in AI use, teaching or assessment, about 45 minutes.

What we promise

  • Participation is voluntary, and you may stop at any time.
  • We do not ask for your name or email.
  • Results are reported only in aggregate, and we never display results for a specific institution.
  • You may ask us to remove a response you submitted.

Review and oversight

The studies are reviewed by the Anne Arundel Community College Institutional Review Board. [IRB determination and protocol number].

Contacts

Questions about the studies: info@workforce2100.ai. Questions about your rights as a participant: AACC IRB, priairb@aacc.edu.
