Summary

Browsing the site does not require an account, and we do not use cookies for advertising or tracking. We collect information only when you take part in a survey or email us, and we report survey results only in aggregate.

1. Who is responsible

The site is operated by Horizon 2100 LLC. Survey responses are collected for research studies conducted by Dr. Angelo E. Thalassinidis under the oversight of the Anne Arundel Community College Institutional Review Board (IRB).

2. What we collect

  • Fluency Check. Your answers and score. If you choose to add your result to the national picture: institution name, institution type, state, enrollment size and, optionally, your role. We do not ask for your name or email.
  • Expert review. Your ratings and comments, your area of expertise, field and years of experience. We do not ask for your name unless you choose to provide contact details.
  • Email. If you email us, your address and message.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, records visitors' IP addresses for security and operations. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address. We do not run analytics or advertising trackers.

3. Storage in your browser

The Fluency Check keeps a random response number in your browser for the current session, so that an update replaces your earlier submission. The expert review saves your progress in your browser so you can return to it. Neither is used for tracking, and both stay on your device; you can clear them in your browser settings.

4. How we use it

To run the surveys, produce aggregate results, improve our research instruments and reply to messages. We never publish or share results that identify a specific institution or person.

5. Where it is stored

Survey responses are sent to Google Apps Script and stored in Google Sheets accessible only to the research team. Responses are kept for one year and then deleted, unless the study protocol requires otherwise; aggregate results may be kept and published.

6. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with service providers that host the site and store responses (GitHub, Google), when required by law, or in aggregate form that identifies no one.

7. Your choices

Survey participation is voluntary. You can ask us to access, correct or delete a response you submitted by emailing info@workforce2100.ai with the approximate date and your institution name. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law.

8. Children

The site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

9. Changes

We will post changes here and update the effective date.
