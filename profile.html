    College profiles

    According to our model and today's data, how might AI change the work your graduates go into? Each profile covers one of 5,668 institutions: its local labor market, its programs, a stress test, the institutions that train for the same jobs, and the adults in the area who may need retraining.

    Example: Anne Arundel Community College · Columbus State Community College · Austin Community College

    Free and open, from public federal data. Estimates, not predictions or advice. Explore the national data · Methods

    Updates

    Updated quarterly

    AI is changing faster than the data that measure it. These profiles are updated quarterly as new federal data are released, each update is dated, and the full model is re-run once a year.

    Every quarter. New federal data as they are released, such as county jobs by industry (BLS QCEW) for the Local industries section and how fast employers are adopting AI.
    Every year. When the BLS employment projections, OEWS wages and IPEDS completions arrive, the full model is re-run: labor markets, programs, the program map, the stress test and competition.
    Not included yet. Robots and other physical automation, wages responding to AI, and local plans such as a new plant or a base closing.
