OpenFor experts in AI use, teaching or assessment

Expert Review: Calibrated Delegation Assessment

Help us validate a new assessment of AI fluency: how well a person decides how much work to hand to an AI system, under what limits, and how to check the result. You judge 30 short scenarios and two performance tasks. Your ratings decide which scenarios are kept, revised or dropped.

Time
About 45 minutes; saved as you go
You do
Pick the best and worst action, rate relevance, flag problems
We ask
Your expertise, field and years of experience

Work 2035 Tracking Study

A multi-year study comparing the Work 2035 forecasts with what actually happens in participating institutions' programs and local job markets, with one short leadership survey a year. Recruitment opens after ethics review, expected in spring 2027.
