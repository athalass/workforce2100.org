Expert Review: Calibrated Delegation Assessment

Help us validate a new assessment of AI fluency: how well a person decides how much work to hand to an AI system, under what limits, and how to check the result. You judge 30 short scenarios and two performance tasks. Your ratings decide which scenarios are kept, revised or dropped.
